## Key findings (paraphrased)

### A) Specialized Profiles sunset (critical timeline)
- Upwork states Specialized Profiles will no longer be available starting **May 28, 2026**.
- Work history / portfolio / earnings context tied to specialized profiles are preserved in main profile display logic.
- However, **title, overview, and skills from specialized profiles do not auto-transfer** to main profile.
- Action implied: manually migrate any specialized-profile messaging into main profile before cutoff.

### B) Main profile optimization guidance
- Keep profile current, relevant, and selective (especially portfolio quality over quantity).
- Avoid exaggeration/misleading claims and avoid contact info on profile/portfolio content.
- For skills: use accurate spellings, relevant synonyms where needed, and maintain meaningful ordering.

### C) Portfolio mechanics and limits
- Portfolio field limits from help guidance:
  - Title up to 70 chars
  - Role up to 100 chars
  - Description up to 600 chars
  - Up to 5 skills/deliverables
- Draft flow exists; publishing is explicit.
- Related contract linking can trigger client review/approval workflow.

### D) Certifications policy shifts
- Upwork notes **Credly sync no longer supported**.
- Legacy Credly certifications may remain as static/read-only depending on prior consent.
- Manual certification entry remains available; custom certifications are possible but not verified badge-equivalent.

### E) Professional licenses
- Upwork distinguishes licenses from certifications; licenses demonstrate legal authorization where required.
- Expected data points: profession, licensing body, license number/N-A, verification URL, issue date, expiration (or no-expiration flag).
- Expired licenses are hidden from client display.

### F) Release-note highlights relevant to freelancers
- Feedback/rating flow simplification updates (single public star flow and private-note changes across periods).
- Partner certification self-serve update appears in Mar 2026 notes.
- Rising Talent criteria updates (verification/payment readiness and criteria clarity changes across 2025 updates).
- Identity verification badge availability appears in 2025 notes.
- Messaging/video meeting and productivity tooling enhancements continue to roll out.
