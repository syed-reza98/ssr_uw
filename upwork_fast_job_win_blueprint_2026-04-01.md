## 7) Proposal Framework for Higher Win Rate

Use this 5-part structure:
1. Hook in first 2 lines (state the client’s problem)
2. Proof of similar delivery (1–2 matching projects)
3. Your implementation approach (short bullets)
4. Timeline + communication cadence
5. CTA with one specific next step

Compliance reminder:
- Do not include personal contact details before contract start.
