## Key findings (paraphrased)

### A) Specialized Profiles sunset (critical timeline)

- Upwork states Specialized Profiles will no longer be available starting **May 28, 2026**.
- Work history / portfolio / earnings context tied to specialized profiles are preserved in main profile display logic.
- However, **title, overview, and skills from specialized profiles do not auto-transfer** to main profile.
- Action implied: manually migrate any specialized-profile messaging into main profile before cutoff.

### B) Main profile optimization guidance

- Keep profile current, relevant, and selective (especially portfolio quality over quantity).
- Avoid exaggeration/misleading claims and avoid contact info on profile/portfolio content.
- For skills: use accurate spellings, relevant synonyms where needed, and maintain meaningful ordering.

### C) Portfolio mechanics and limits

- Portfolio field limits from help guidance:
  - Title up to 70 chars
  - Role up to 100 chars
  - Description up to 600 chars
  - Up to 5 skills/deliverables
- Draft flow exists; publishing is explicit.
- Related contract linking can trigger client review/approval workflow.

### D) Certifications policy shifts

- Upwork notes **Credly sync no longer supported**.
- Legacy Credly certifications may remain as static/read-only depending on prior consent.
- Manual certification entry remains available; custom certifications are possible but not verified badge-equivalent.

### E) Professional licenses

- Upwork distinguishes licenses from certifications; licenses demonstrate legal authorization where required.
- Expected data points: profession, licensing body, license number/N-A, verification URL, issue date, expiration (or no-expiration flag).
- Expired licenses are hidden from client display.

### F) Release-note highlights relevant to freelancers

- Feedback/rating flow simplification updates (single public star flow and private-note changes across periods).
- Partner certification self-serve update appears in Mar 2026 notes.
- Rising Talent criteria updates (verification/payment readiness and criteria clarity changes across 2025 updates).
- Identity verification badge availability appears in 2025 notes.
- Messaging/video meeting and productivity tooling enhancements continue to roll out.

## Update: 2026-04-20 (Getting hired as a new software-dev freelancer)

### Sources reviewed (official)

**Profile + discoverability**

- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024526754-How-to-add-categories-to-your-freelancer-profile
  - Categories help clients find you; pick ones aligned with the work you want to win.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053305673-How-to-choose-a-good-profile-picture
  - Photo must clearly show your face; avoid disallowed formats (logos/watermarks/obscured face).
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/20850487528723-How-to-boost-your-profile
  - Boosted Profile is paid visibility in search; you set targeting and per-click spend caps.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403673074707-Availability-Badge
  - Availability Badge is a paid “available now” signal; weekly spend varies and can be capped.

**Proposals + pricing**

- https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-to-create-a-proposal-that-wins-jobs
  - Proposal structure guidance (hook, needs, solution, proof, timeline, costs, CTA); avoid sharing contact info pre-contract.
- https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-to-set-your-freelance-rate
  - Rate-setting guidance: work backward from desired income and billable time; validate against market rates; pick a pricing model.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/34019683309587-Proposal-insights
  - Proposal insights (when available) help contextualize competition and client activity.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406541109011-Boosted-Proposals
  - Boosting uses extra Connects to place proposals into boosted slots the client sees first.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406395531795-Boost-your-proposal
  - Boost mechanics: bid Connects for placement; treat as an optional, selective strategy.

**Communication rules + safety (high-risk policy topics)**

- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051749534-How-to-keep-your-contact-information-safe-on-Upwork
  - Don’t share personal contact info pre-contract; limited exceptions exist; report suspicious messages.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052511833-Get-to-know-each-other-before-a-contract
  - Interview/scoping should occur on Upwork tools; pre-contract off-platform contact/payment can trigger restrictions/badge loss.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052511133-Circumvention-and-why-it-s-against-the-rules
  - Circumvention = off-platform contact/payment pre-contract; Upwork describes penalties and compliant alternatives.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500007951322-How-to-communicate-professionally-on-Upwork
  - Professional conduct expectations include avoiding abuse and plagiarism; be clear and responsive.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211067668-How-to-stay-safe-on-Upwork
  - Common scam red flags include off-platform payment/contact, fees, and free-work requests; report suspicious activity.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/34385274054547-Scams-Frequently-Asked-Questions
  - Upwork emphasizes it cannot recover funds paid off-platform; use Upwork payments to stay protected.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051800034-What-to-do-if-someone-contacts-you-outside-Upwork
  - If contacted externally using info from Upwork, Upwork advises reporting rather than engaging off-platform.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/7180643124243-What-is-feedback-building
  - “Feedback building” patterns (fake work/review manipulation) are prohibited and can trigger suspension.

**Fees**

- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee
  - Freelancer service fee is per-contract and can vary by contract; shown before acceptance and then locked for that contract.

**Payment protection + time tracking**

- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211064098-Log-time-with-Time-Tracker
  - Official time tracker logs time in segments with screenshots and activity levels; memos matter; manual time isn’t protected.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068288-How-Hourly-Payment-Protection-works-for-freelancers
  - Hourly protection depends on tracker usage, meaningful memos, sufficient activity, and staying within policy limits.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063748-How-Fixed-Price-Payment-Protection-works-for-freelancers-on-Upwork
  - Fixed-price protection requires funded milestones and correct “Submit work for payment” flow; avoid working unfunded.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023544173-How-to-propose-or-respond-to-milestone-changes-as-a-freelancer
  - Use the milestone-change workflow; don’t work changed scope until accepted (and funded if needed).
- https://www.upwork.com/legal#fp
  - Fixed-price legal escrow instructions define the formal release/dispute mechanics and timelines.

**Disputes**

- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068588-What-to-do-if-a-client-disputes-your-hours-on-Upwork
  - Hourly disputes can pause the contract; Upwork reviews the work diary against protection requirements if you decline the dispute.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068528-Dispute-non-release-of-a-milestone-payment
  - Fixed-price non-release disputes have timeboxed steps; evidence submission is required; may escalate to arbitration.

**Negotiation workflow**

- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/34262508186899-How-to-request-changes-to-an-offer
  - “Request changes” enables in-platform negotiation of key terms with visible history and offer time windows.

**Job-post quality + prohibited jobs**

- https://www.upwork.com/resources/spotting-fake-job-posts
  - Resource Center guidance on spotting low-quality/fake postings and reducing scam exposure.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500007578942-What-kind-of-jobs-aren-t-allowed-on-Upwork
  - Upwork disallows job posts that require free work (e.g., contests/unpaid internships) and other prohibited categories.

**Project Catalog**

- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058234233-How-to-get-started-with-Project-Catalog-as-a-freelancer
  - Project Catalog supports productized services with review/publish flow and visibility on your profile.

**JSS + badges**

- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068358-All-about-your-Job-Success-Score
  - JSS is based on outcomes/client satisfaction over rolling windows; recalculated daily.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/38437458199059-How-is-my-Job-Success-Score-calculated
  - Upwork describes a high-level success vs negative outcomes model and “best-of window” display rule.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063228-How-to-become-a-Rising-Talent-on-Upwork
  - Rising Talent is an Upwork-assigned badge for newer freelancers meeting eligibility criteria.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068468-How-to-become-Top-Rated-on-Upwork
  - Top Rated eligibility includes sustained strong outcomes and policy compliance.
- https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049625454-What-is-Expert-Vetted-status-on-Upwork
  - Expert-Vetted is an invite/screening program with benefits for higher-tier clients.
